About us

Trusted Industrial Mineral Supplier

AAPAS Salt & Minerals was founded with a vision to bridge the gap between natural mineral resources and industrial demand. Rooted in Gujarat — one of India’s leading mineral regions — we supply premium-quality minerals with a strong focus on purity, consistency, reliability, and large-scale availability.

We serve chemical industries, ceramics manufacturers, drilling operations, exporters, and industrial processing units with specification-grade materials designed to support long-term industrial growth. Our commitment is not only to deliver minerals, but to build trusted partnerships through quality and dependable supply.
